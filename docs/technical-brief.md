# Technical Brief: "Electromagnetism in the Wild"

## Faraday’s Law Explained for Everyone
Faraday discovered that **moving magnets create electricity**. In a transformer, we don’t move magnets — we use alternating current (AC) to make the magnetic field constantly change direction and strength.

**Simple version**:  
If you wave a magnet near a wire, electricity appears in the wire. The faster or stronger the change, the more electricity.

## Lenz’s Law (The Rebel Rule)
Nature doesn’t like change. When the magnetic flux tries to increase through the secondary coil, the coil creates its own magnetic field to *oppose* that change. This opposition is what actually drives usable current.

## Why Iron Core?
Iron concentrates magnetic field lines (high permeability), dramatically increasing the flux Φ_B and therefore the induced voltage.
